KismoEngine Commercial License (summary)
========================================

A commercial licence is for organisations that want to embed KismoEngine in closed products,
keep modifications private, or run hosted services without the AGPL-3.0 network obligations.

Request process:
Open an issue using the "Commercial License Request" template:
Title: Commercial License Request: <Your Organization Name>
Include: organisation, intended use (embedded product / game / hosted service / internal tool),
whether modifications stay private, expected scale, desired term, any required clauses, and a
target decision date.

Do NOT put confidential information in the public issue. Ask to move to a private channel.

Proposed terms (overview, not legally binding):
- Grant: non-exclusive worldwide licence to use, modify, embed and distribute in proprietary form.
- Copyleft waiver: no obligation to release derivative source under AGPL.
- Attribution: optional "Built with KismoEngine" (can be waived).
- Fees: tiered by distribution model, scale or revenue.
- Support and updates: available as options during an active term.
- Liability: standard disclaimer and limitation of liability.

Final rights and obligations are defined only in an executed agreement.
